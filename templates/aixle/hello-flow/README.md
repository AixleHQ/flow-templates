# Hello Flow

The starter template of the Aixle Flow product tour, part of the getting-started onboarding. Put a company name on a card, drag it into a column, and about three minutes later the card holds a one-page HTML profile of that company, researched from verified sources. No secrets, integrations or repositories needed.

## What you get

- `<company>-one-pager.html`: a self-contained one-page profile that opens offline, in light or dark mode, and prints well. It covers what the company does, key facts (founded, headquarters, employees, revenue, ownership, CEO), products and services, customers and market, recent business news, and every source with a link.
- The file is attached to the card and saved in the project's Assets.

## How it works

```
Card enters Researching  →  Research the company  →  Lay out the one-pager  →  Card in Done
                             verified sources          fixed HTML template
```

- **Start from the board.** Create a card titled with a company name (add the website when the name is ambiguous, for example "Acme, acme.com") in To research and drag it to Researching.
- **Verified sources only.** The company's own site and filings first, then independent business press from more than one outlet. No Wikipedia, no forums, no syndication copies, and only pages the agent actually read.
- **Business only.** Results, products, customers, deals and leadership changes. No personal stories about people.
- **English output.** The profile, the card comments and the run notes are in English, whatever language the user's agent normally speaks.
- **Clear when it can't help.** If the card title isn't a company name, or no such company exists, the card gets a comment saying why and moves to Needs fixing. Rename it and drag it back to Researching.

## What it cannot do

- Deep research: it is a quick profile, about three minutes of searching.
- Facts a company does not publish and the press does not report: they show as "Not public".
- Paywalled or blocked pages: the agent leaves those facts out rather than guessing.

## Setup

Activate the **Researching → Hello Flow** trigger on the template's setup checklist (triggers install inactive). Nothing else is needed.
